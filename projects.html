Predicting churn before it happens

The problem

Subscribers were canceling with no warning signs the support team could act on. By the time someone reached out to cancel, it was already too late to change their mind with a single retention offer.

What I did

Pulled 18 months of product usage, support tickets, and billing events into a single feature set. Trained a gradient-boosted model to score every active subscriber weekly on churn risk 30 days out, then worked with lifecycle marketing to route high-risk users into a targeted save campaign instead of a blanket discount that eroded margin.

  • Engineered 40+ behavioral features from raw event logs
  • Validated with time-based cross-validation to avoid leakage
  • Built a Looker dashboard so CS could see risk scores per account

Result

The flagged segment saw a 17% reduction in churn over the following quarter compared to a held-out control group, while overall discount spend dropped since offers were only sent where they were likely to matter.
